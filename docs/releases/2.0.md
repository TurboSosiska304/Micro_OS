# Micro OS 2.0

> The machine of its user: a program that cannot be written by another, a
> `fork` that is a copy and not a re-run, two people on one machine, a desktop
> that starts with the machine, a network with an address nobody typed, and a
> stick that keeps a file when the power goes.

1.9 gave the machine its files: a journal, a guard page, a terminal and the
order of a clean end. 2.0 is the release that lets the machine be **shared and
trusted** - and the first release of the line that was tested on a real laptop
by its owner, in the first minute of the first image, which is where six of the
defects below came from.

## The policy of the MMU (ADR 0052)

The page tables of a program exist since 1.0 (ADR 0007). What 2.0 adds is the
**policy**, and it is the hardware that enforces it:

* `mmap` of `PROT_WRITE|PROT_EXEC` is refused with `EINVAL` **before** a page
  exists, and the audit of `exec` stays as the second lock;
* the stack **grows** on the fault of its guard page, up to the ceiling of the
  task (`ulimit -m`), with the base read from the tables and not from a guess;
* `brk` and the anonymous mappings are charged against the same ceiling, and
  the heap of the kernel (calls 20-22) is not a program's to take any more;
* the kernel is unreachable from ring 3: the supervisor bit on every machine,
  and **SMEP and SMAP** where the processor has them, with a window around
  each copy (`k_port_exec_user_access_begin/end`, the contract of the port
  **1.14**);
* a bad access answers `EFAULT` with its address in the log.

`make mmu-smoke` is 52 checks on a processor with SMEP and SMAP and 51 on one
without: a program that writes its text, executes its stack, writes a kernel
address, grows past its heap ceiling or touches a page it does not own ends
with `EFAULT` - the **task** ends, the machine answers the next line.

## `fork` is a copy of the pages (ADR 0057)

The copy of a process is a copy of its pages and not a re-run of its program,
and it is cheap: a program of 64 MiB forks in a millisecond (`make fork-smoke`).

The lane also closed a hole the first lane of MMU opened: the AC flag of SMAP
was restored from the context a switch was going *to* instead of the one on the
core, which would have made a `write` under SMAP fault in ring 0.

## The accounts of a person (ADR 0044, ADR 0053)

* `chgrp` is the call **51** of the ABI (the count is 52) with an adapter in
  every filesystem - MicroFS, ext2, and `ENOTSUP` on FAT32, which has no group;
* the **group of a record** is in `struct vfs_stat`, and `struct statx` is
  still 40 bytes (the group lives in the hole after `type`);
* one rule of the triads (`vfs_access_bits()`) answers for the VFS and for the
  edge of the calls, so the two cannot disagree;
* `/etc/group` with its parser and `getgrgid`/`getgrnam`, and a new object
  takes the gid of its maker;
* the **setuid bit of MOX1**: a flag of the header, the real, effective and
  saved uid of the task, the act in `execve` that refuses an image its caller
  can write, and `pkg` that installs such a package only as root.
  `/bin/suidcat` reads a file of its owner, the same program without the bit is
  refused, a writable setuid image is refused, and the shell keeps its own
  identity after the run.

## The desk of a person (ADR 0054)

The machine boots into the desktop: `init` starts the session, `/etc/session`
runs `/bin/wm`, and the prompt returns when the last window closes. The window
of the **terminal** runs a command of `/bin` on two pipes - the keys go to it,
`Ctrl+C` is a signal, `Ctrl+T` is the second size of the font (16x32), `Ctrl+Q`
leaves. The clock of the panel is the time of the machine in the format of
`/etc/console`, and a second of it repaints **only the rectangle of the hour**.

The lane found a defect of 1.7 on the way: the damage of a window was compared
with the edges of the frame instead of the rectangle, so one key repainted
1280x765 - it is 640x16 now, and two screenshots around a key hold it. The
numbers of the compositor: the first frame 1 024 000 px in 5 ms, three keys
3 % of the screen with the worst frame at 3 ms, a still desk 0 px.

## The wire of a person (ADR 0055)

**DHCP** as a client of the library: DISCOVER/OFFER/REQUEST/ACK, T1 and T2, a
NAK that starts again, the lease in `/etc/network` (read before the request,
written after the answer, `dhcp 0` for the fixed address of 1.8), and a
fallback that keeps the machine usable when no server answers. And the
**Ethernet of a laptop**: `e1000e` (the I219 of a ThinkPad and the 82574L of
QEMU), with a 64-bit BAR read from both halves, a window that answers all ones
refused as "not a card", and a window the port could not map not registered at
all - the frame path asks whether the map is there and drops the frame instead
of faulting.

## The stick of a person is a disk (ADR 0056)

The image of `make uefi-stick` is a table of **two partitions**: the ESP of
64 MiB with the loader and the kernel, and a second partition of **MicroFS** of
24 MiB. The kernel finds that partition over xHCI and its class of mass
storage (`usb0p2`) and mounts it **read-write**, and the door of the writes
opens because the medium is ours and `/etc/volumes` names it. The module the
loader carries is a copy in memory: it is **read-only and says so** - before
this release it said "read-write" and answered `EIO` to the first write.

## The defects a real laptop found

The owner booted the first image of 1.9.0 on a ThinkPad X1 Carbon and found, in
minutes, what a simulator cannot:

* **a serial port that is not there answers `0xFF`** and the console read it as
  a key - a byte that prints nothing - until the line of the shell was full:
  every line answered "line too long", and the keyboard of the PS/2 and of the
  USB was never asked;
* the **scratchpad buffers** of xHCI: a controller of QEMU asks for none, the
  controller of a laptop for 65, and the driver kept 8 - no USB at all;
* the **volume of the boot medium** is `boot0p1` and the image did not name it,
  so the rule of ADR 0041 made the stick read-only;
* the **ACPI tables above the gigabyte** were walked through physical addresses
  after `paging_init` had cut the identity map to the first gigabyte: the clean
  end did all its work and then the machine faulted instead of switching off;
* the **Ethernet window of a 64-bit BAR** was mapped by the lower half;
* the port read the **year of the CMOS through BCD twice**: 2026 was printed as
  2020 and 2043 as 2031.

Each one is a case or a line of a gate now.

## What is not in 2.0

* **Wi-Fi** (`docs/plan-wifi.md`, ADR 0039): it follows the wire, and it is a
  lane of its own;
* a **`pty`** and a userland shell in the window of the terminal (debt K11): the
  window runs a program on two pipes, and the shell of the machine is a
  function of the kernel, unreachable from ring 3;
* the **ARM port** (`docs/plan-arm64.md`, a later release, no date);
* **3D** and a driver of a real GPU: the compositor is content with
  `k_fb_fill`/`k_fb_blit`, and the beginning of a GPU is a promise of 3.0;
* **MQTT**, stopped for the x86 port in 1.6.
