# Micro OS 1.7

The release of the **desktop**, of the **disk at the speed of the medium**, of a
**filesystem that does not walk a table of a gigabyte**, and of a machine that
**starts on a laptop of these years from a stick**.

The board stays the compatibility port of ADR 0018: it builds, passes its
device test and receives the cheap changes.

## A desktop

ADR 0028 said the interface is a program with two devices; 1.7 gives it a
window system.

* a **pointer**: the second port of the 8042 and IRQ 12, events of
  `/dev/input0` beside the keys (`key.pointer.x`, `key.pointer.y`,
  `key.button.left`, appended and never moved);
* **`/bin/wm`**, the server, in ring 3: the wallpaper, a panel, the pointer, a
  list of windows with a focus, and a repaint of **only the rectangles that
  changed**; a click raises, the bar drags, the cross and `Alt+F4` close, the
  panel puts a window away and back, and the window of a program that ended is
  taken away with `waitpid(WNOHANG)`;
* a **protocol** of 60-byte messages (`docs/desktop.md`) over a pipe, and a
  **shared buffer** per window through a `MAP_SHARED` file - this machine has
  no shared memory object and this is one, with `msync` before the message;
* **`components/ui/client/`**, a portable client of the server, tested on the
  host, so that `/bin/edit` and `/bin/fm` became windows **without their logic
  being rewritten**: without `MICROOS_WM` they still take the whole screen;
* **`kernel/fb_ops.h`** - the fills and blits a device may do itself - with the
  software path as the only implementation. A contract and no driver: the 2D
  engine of 1.8 plugs in here, and a driver without a caller is a driver nobody
  has measured.

`make x86-vga-smoke`: **164** checks on pixels, including two windows, a click
that raises one, keys that reach the focus, a drag that moves a rectangle, a
close that hands the focus back, and the screen returned to the kernel.

## The disk and the filesystems

**ext2 writes in runs of the medium** (ADR 0037): a cursor of the allocation
per group, a bitmap and a descriptor marked dirty and settled at the end of a
run, a file read and written 64 KiB at a time, and **no zeroing of a block that
a write is about to fill**.

| Volume of 5 GiB | 1.6.0 | 1.7.0 |
|---|---|---|
| ext2, write 128 MiB | 14.0 MiB/s | **125-147 MiB/s** |
| ext2, read | 35 | 83 (a pure read: 185) |
| `make bench`, ext2 write 16 MiB | 15 | **246 MiB/s** |

**MicroFS version 3** (ADR 0038): the table of the records is sized by the
records a volume needs, not 4 % of it.

| 5 GiB volume | 1.6.0 | 1.7.0 |
|---|---|---|
| `META` | 214 MB | **660 blocks** (660 KB) |
| `mount` to the shell | 13.8 s | **0.3 s** |
| `df /` | 14.8 s | **0.07 s** |
| records in the table | 6 689 887 | 319 |

A record of version 3 carries a **CRC32** (60 of its 64 bytes), so `fsck`
tells a torn record from a whole one and puts a volume right after a reset in
the middle of a write; and it carries the **group** of the object. A volume of
version 1 and 2 still mounts, reads and writes. `make x86-mfs-smoke` kills
QEMU in the middle of a write and checks the file before it.

`stextx`'s `group` landed in the room the alignment of the structure already
had: the call keeps its 40 bytes and **an image of 1.0 still runs** - the gate
of the frozen image refused the first attempt, which made the structure grow.

## The second disk, and the read ahead

**virtio-blk** (ADR 0037) beside the IDE channel: the queues of the network
driver, one chain of descriptors per request, eight in flight, the interrupt
of the line, and a fallback to IDE when the device is not there. The image is
the same and the bus is a property of the machine (`X86_DISK_BUS=virtio`).

A device now says how much read ahead it wants, and both disks of the port ask
for 128 sectors:

| | 1.6.0 | 1.7.0 |
|---|---|---|
| MicroFS, read 16 MiB | 134 MiB/s | **262 MiB/s** |
| ext2, read | 51 | 62 |

The write did not move on virtio, and that is the finding of the release: the
device is a hundredth of the time of a file (`make bench` says 14 ms of the
medium against 1068 ms of the call), and the ceiling is **the grain of the
disk** - five sector operations per kilobyte written. That is the lane of
**4 KiB blocks**, written down in `docs/debts.md` with its five steps and not
started here.

## The machine of a person: a firmware, a stick, a laptop

ADR 0037 of the port, and the first release that starts on a machine that is
not QEMU.

* **`ports/port_x86_64/loader/uefi/`** - a PE/COFF application of 16 KiB with
  the tables of the firmware written by hand: **GOP** for the picture (1920x1080
  of 32 bpp on a laptop of 2018), `EFI_SIMPLE_FILE_SYSTEM` for the kernel and
  the volume, the **memory map**, `ExitBootServices`, and the **RSDP of the
  configuration table** - under a firmware it is not in the EBDA;
* the kernel is the same one the BIOS path runs, with the hand-off of the
  picture and the address of the image; `src/gop.c` keeps a shadow of RGB565
  and converts a band of cells at a time;
* **the heap and the modules out of the window of a program**: a loader may put
  a module at 35 MiB, and 32-48 MiB is the arena a program sees through its
  page tables - the kernel now copies such a module out of the window before
  the page tables exist, and places the heap in the first free piece outside
  it. The 600-second session of 1.6 found the shape of this bug from the other
  side; a laptop found this one.

| gate | checks |
|---|---|
| `make uefi` | a FAT volume with `BOOTX64.EFI`, the kernel and the disk |
| `make uefi-stick` | the same in a table of partitions: a stick written with `dd` |
| `make uefi-smoke` | **26**, under OVMF: the picture of GOP, the disk, the shell |
| `make x86-iso-smoke` / `-usb-smoke` / `-uefi-smoke` | **14 / 13 / 26**: one image of a CD and of a stick, in a BIOS and in a firmware |

The release carries both mediums: `MicroOS-<version>.iso` (a CD, a BIOS, a
stick) and `MicroOS-<version>-uefi.img` (a stick of a machine of UEFI only).

**A ThinkPad X1 Carbon of 2018 boots it**: `the boot is uefi`, `the screen is
the framebuffer of the firmware, 1920x1080, 32 bpp bgrx`, `8 cpu(s) reported by
the firmware`, `root: the root is mfs on boot0p1`, `/bin/hello` in ring 3 and
the shell. The keyboard of that machine speaks the scancode set 2 and this
release speaks set 1: the keys arrive and the line is refused as too long.
That defect is the first of 1.7.1, and it is why a release on hardware is worth
a release in a virtual machine.

## The defects the release found

* the **cleanroom** found an account bypass - create, unlink and rename never
  asked for the write bit of the directory, and a task of uid 7 deleted
  `/etc/passwd` and wrote its own `/etc/shadow` - and a **cwd cut in the middle
  of a name**, where `rm` then removed the file of another directory. Both are
  L1 and both are fixed;
* the **storage of an image** of 12-16 MiB no longer has to fit below 16 MiB:
  the loader moves a module that lies over the copy of the kernel instead of
  refusing the boot;
* a **`bench` that says when the host was too busy** to be a number.

## What is not in 1.7

* a block of 4 KiB, and the runs of the filesystem that follow it;
* the journal of ext2, and a page cache;
* USB (a keyboard of a laptop, a stick as a disk), AHCI and NVMe, a writable
  root of a real machine;
* the second half of the accounts, and the socket in the ABI;
* WiFi: `docs/plan-wifi.md` and ADR 0039 - the BSD driver `iwm` with
  `net80211`, after the disk, the wire and USB, and not in this release;
* the ARM port: a later release, and the plan names no version.
