# The cleanroom of 2.0.2

* Date: 26 November 2026
* Tree: tag `v2.0.2` (`deab3e41`), a scratch worktree, no changes committed
* Machine: the host simulator (`make sim`) and the separate volumes of `/tmp`;
  the QEMU journeys were stopped and are not covered here (see below)
* What it did: a person who knows only the wiki - first boot, `help`, every
  command with `-h`, files, pipes and redirects, errors, `config`, `mkfs`,
  HTTP - 18 simulator sessions, `make smoke` 70 of 70

The findings are the L1-L4 list of `docs/plan-2.1.md` ("The cleanroom pass").
They are recorded as **K29** in `docs/debts.md` and fixed in
`feature/k29-cleanroom`; this page is the evidence and the shape of the
procedure, not a second list to keep in step.

## The findings

1. **L1 - `rm /` answers "use -r", and `rm -r /` deletes the volume.** The
   walk reached every file it could (`/proc/version` and `/dev` answered
   "operation not supported" and the walk went on) and `ls /` was left with
   `proc/` and `dev/` only: `etc/` and `home/` were gone. The hint of the shell
   led to the loss.
2. **L2 - `sleep -1` blocks for sixty seconds** and reports
   `18446744073709551615 ms is too much, slept 60000 ms`.
3. **L2/L3 - `echo` with eight arguments** answers "too many arguments in one
   stage", while `docs/wiki/troubleshooting.md` promises that the tail of the
   line is ignored.
4. **L3 - `http get` of a 600-byte body** answers `HTTP 200, 256 byte(s)` and a
   body cut in the middle with no word about the truncation; `wget` of the same
   URL answers 600 bytes.
5. **L3 - `help` on the simulator** lists `http`, `wget`, `ota`, `ntp`,
   `httpd` and `mqtt`, and `docs/wiki/shell.md` says they are not registered on
   that machine.
6. **L3/L4 - five places where a page and the machine disagree**: `rm -r`
   exists (two pages say it does not), `mount` prints `none on / type ...` and
   not only the mount points, the examples of `/tmp` and `wc` do not run on the
   simulator, `config set display.rotation 400` keeps 400 where the page
   promises a fall back to the default, and `config sync` plus `mkfs -y` in one
   session lose `/etc` until the next boot.

## What was not covered

The QEMU journeys - the desk from the shell (`exec /bin/wm`, `Alt+T`, `help`,
`Alt+F4`), the ramfs of `/tmp` on a read-only root and the network - were
prepared and not run: the emulator was held by another lane, the wrapper that
waited for it was stopped, and an orphan QEMU of that attempt is what a later
lane found on the host. They are the first thing the 2.1 pass runs, and the
`x86-iso-desk-smoke` and `log-smoke` gates of 2.1 cover two of the three.

## What the pass says about the procedure

A person with the wiki and the machine finds in an hour what a gate of 33
checks did not: the gates prove the promises the tree chose to check, and the
cleanroom proves the promises a person reads. Both are needed; the second one
is what the release notes of 2.1 will be written from.
