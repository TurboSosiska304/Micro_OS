# The author of this tree

Micro OS is written by its owner and by the people who contribute to it, and the
copyright of the tree is theirs together (`LICENSE`).

An **AI assistant is a tool, not an author.** Code, text and tests that an
assistant helped to produce are the work of the person who directed it: the
assistant has no copyright interest in this tree and cannot hold one, and its
name is not a party to the license. A `Co-authored-by:` trailer that names a
model is a convention of a tool and not a claim of authorship; two commits of
the 2.1 history carry one, and this file is the record that they change nothing
about who owns the tree.

The rule that follows, for a person and for an agent alike:

* a commit is signed by the person who made it; `Co-authored-by:` names a person
  who wrote part of the change, never a model, an editor or a script;
* the history describes the change and not the tools that helped to type it;
* the license of the tree is the grant of its authors, and a change of it is a
  decision of theirs alone.

The facts about the code of other projects are another matter and are recorded
where they are used: `docs/licensing.md` and the header of each file that names
a source (`docs/adr/0058-the-language-of-the-tree.md` and the GPU ports are the
examples).
